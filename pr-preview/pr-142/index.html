    Questions?

    Straight answers from the porcelain trading floor.

    Does Poop Profit pay me to poop?
    No. You are already getting paid to poop when you poop during work. We just do the math and keep the receipts. The money was always there, sitting in the dark, uncounted, like a forgotten 401k.
    How does it work?
    Enter your hourly rate or your salary. Hit start when you sit. Hit stop when you stand. Poop Profit handles the rest.
    Do I need an account?
    No. It is free and works with zero sign up. A free account saves your log across devices and unlocks Turd Tokens, but if you are reading this from inside a stall right now, just hit start. We can handle the paperwork later.
    Does it work if I am salaried?
    Yes. Switch to salary mode, enter your annual salary and weekly hours, and Poop Profit converts it into a real hourly rate. Salaried employees consistently report the strongest emotional reaction to this number. Several have described it as a turning point.
    Are bathroom breaks actually paid time?
    In most workplaces, bathroom breaks during the workday are paid time. Policies vary by employer.
    How much does the average person earn per bathroom break?
    That depends on your rate and your pace. Some users are sprinters and pushes. Some users treat it as a wellness retreat. Poop Profit tracks your personal averages, best session, and lifetime total so you can stop estimating and start knowing.
    How much do I get paid to poop at work per year?
    Take your hourly pay, multiply it by the minutes you spend on the toilet each workday, and divide by 60. That is your daily take. Multiply by 250 workdays and you have the year. At $25 an hour and 10 minutes a day, that comes to about $1,042 a year. That is the generic math, though. Your real number depends on your actual visits, which Poop Profit times to the second.
    Does Poop Profit work in pounds, euros, or other currencies?
    Yes. Poop is universal, and so is Poop Profit. It picks your currency automatically from your location, and you can switch it any time in Wage Settings. It works in 28 currencies, including British pounds, euros, Canadian and Australian dollars, Indian rupees, Brazilian reais, and Mexican pesos. It only changes the symbol, not the math: enter your pay in your own currency and Poop Profit does the rest.
    Can I track more than one session per day?
    Yes. There is no daily limit. Log every single one. The stats page will not judge your fiber intake or your fast food decisions.
    Can I share my stats?
    Yes. Every session and every stat summary can be shared to social, messages, or copied as text. Whether your group chat was ready for this information is between you and your group chat.
    What are Turd Tokens?
    Turd Tokens are the official Poop Profit currency, earned through activity, streaks, and milestones. They live in your wallet and will unlock games and merch as those roll out.
    Will my boss find out?
    Not from us. Your data is yours.
    Can I use it working from home?
    Yes. Remote workers post some of the highest numbers on the entire platform.
