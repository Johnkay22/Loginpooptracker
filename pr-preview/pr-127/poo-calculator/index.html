How the Maths Works

Here is the whole thing worked through for someone on £30,000 a year who spends 10 minutes a day on the loo.

  1. Step 1

    Find your hourly rate

    £30,000 a year divided by 1,950 paid hours (37.5 hours a week for 52 weeks) comes to £15.38 an hour.

  2. Step 2

    Break it down by the minute

    £15.38 divided by 60 is about 26p for every minute you spend on the loo during working hours.

  3. Step 3

    Add up the year

    10 minutes a day across 232 workdays is 38 hours and 40 minutes on the clock. At £15.38 an hour, that comes to about £595 a year.

Herman calls this the porcelain dividend. HMRC has not commented.
